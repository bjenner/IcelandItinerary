# Project Context

This project is for planning a trip to Iceland in June.

Known trip facts:
- Arrive in Iceland on Saturday, June 13.
- Leave Iceland on Saturday, June 27.
- Rent a 4x4 camper for the entire stay.
- Do not plan hotel stays.
- The camper setup should allow flexibility to explore wherever desired, including F-roads when conditions and rules allow.

When helping with this project:
- Treat the camper as the default accommodation and transportation.
- Favor flexible itineraries, campsite-aware planning, road-condition checks, and weather-aware routing.
- Assume the trip should support spontaneous exploration rather than fixed hotel-based scheduling.
- Flag any Iceland-specific constraints that may affect the plan, especially F-road openings, legal camping rules, weather, river crossings, and vehicle restrictions.

Current planning artifacts:
- `itinerary.md` is the current source of truth for the trip plan.
- `maps/itinerary-map.html` is the interactive companion map for the itinerary.
- Keep the itinerary and map in sync when adding, removing, or significantly changing planning points.
- The map is intentionally a menu of options, not a strict turn-by-turn route.
- Prefer adding useful map layers for recurring trip interests such as campgrounds, hot springs/geothermal pools, special meals, F-roads/highland options, road checks, and optional branches.
- For map planning points, include concise notes, links where helpful, and details that support in-the-moment decision making.
- Track recommendations from people as structured place notes where possible: who recommended it, why, and any context or caution.
- Do not treat recommendations as confirmed itinerary items unless the itinerary says so.
- For future map work, preserve the local interactive browser workflow: serve the project locally if needed so map tiles load correctly in Codex.
