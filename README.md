# Iceland Itinerary

Planning notes, route ideas, and the post-trip archive for a June camper trip around Iceland.

## Trip Snapshot

- **Dates:** June 13-27
- **Style:** 4x4 camper, campground-based, flexible route
- **Main shape:** Counterclockwise Ring Road loop with optional branches
- **Big anchors:** 40th anniversary day on June 14 and summer solstice window on June 20-21

## Sites

This repo keeps both halves of the trip together:

- **Planning site:** `maps/itinerary-map.html` for the flexible route, options, and trip decisions.
- **Travel log site:** `travel-log/index.html` for the actual daily record, places visited, photos, videos, and links.
- **Collection inbox:** `travel-log/collection-inbox.md` for raw source material before it gets curated into the travel log.

## Archive Workflow

Now that the trip is over, start with the collection inbox:

1. Gather raw sources: phone photos, camera files, videos, shared albums, map/location history, messages, receipts, and notes.
2. Drop rough links and reminders into `travel-log/collection-inbox.md` under the matching day.
3. Curate each day into `travel-log/data/days.js`: summary, journal, places, media, links, camp, weather, and road notes.
4. Keep large original media outside Git. Store only selected web-ready images, poster frames, and metadata here.

## Map Layers

The interactive map is meant to be a menu of options, not a strict turn-by-turn route. Current layers include:

- Core route and expanded campground areas
- Food and special meal candidates
- Hot springs and geothermal pools
- Waterfalls and lakes
- Volcanoes and lava fields
- Optional branches, highland roads, and safety/live-check points

## Planning Style

This trip favors good decision-making over locking every day in place. The plan keeps room for weather, road conditions, F-road openings, camper rental restrictions, energy level, and the simple joy of finding something better than the schedule.

Before remote roads, highland detours, volcanic areas, or exposed drives, check:

- Road conditions: https://umferdin.is/en
- Travel safety: https://safetravel.is
- Weather: https://en.vedur.is/
- Volcano monitoring: https://en.vedur.is/volcanoes/

## Working Together

Use `itinerary.md` as the source of truth for the trip plan. When a place is added, removed, or promoted from "maybe" to "likely," update the map too so the itinerary and map stay in sync.
