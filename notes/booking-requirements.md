# Booking Requirements Inventory

Trip dates: June 13-27, 2026  
Purpose: first-pass classification of itinerary/map inventory by whether it needs advance booking.

This is not yet the booking tracker. Later, turn the `Book ahead` and `Reserve recommended` rows into a view that tracks booked/not booked, date, time, confirmation number, cancellation window, and weather-flex fallback.

## Status Key

- `Book ahead`: reserve before the trip or as soon as the date is chosen.
- `Reserve recommended`: walk-up may be possible, but booking is useful for preferred time, high season, limited capacity, or CPAP/power certainty.
- `Call/check ahead`: no normal ticket reservation, but conditions, opening, road access, or facilities should be checked before committing.
- `Walk-up / no booking`: no advance booking expected; may still need parking/payment on site.
- `Logistics only`: city/area/route marker, not a bookable item.
