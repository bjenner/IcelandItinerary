# Vehicle Rental Notes

Trip dates: Saturday, June 13 to Saturday, June 27.

Current target vehicle type: 4x4 camper, likely Toyota Hilux camper or Toyota Land Cruiser camper.

## Shortlist

### Toyota Hilux 4x4 camper

Best fit when interior camper comfort matters: indoor seating, more living space, sink/water, cooking setup, fridge/cooler, heater, bedding/storage, and enough space for bad-weather camping.

Known options:
- Rent.is Toyota Hilux 4x4 Camper: automatic, seats 5, sleeps 4, Webasto heating, sink, fridge, 230V/12V, CDW and unlimited mileage included.
- Hertz Toyota Hilux Camper: manual, 4x4, sleeps 3, highland/F-road suitable when roads are open, CDW/third-party insurance/unlimited mileage included. Optional camping extras may need to be booked separately.
- Lotus Car Rental Toyota Hilux 4x4 Camper: automatic, 2024-2025 model, 4WD, 4 passengers, F-roads allowed, SCDW/third-party/theft/roadside assistance/unlimited mileage listed as included.
- JS Camper Rental Toyota Hilux 4x4: smaller direct specialist fleet, campers for 2-3 adults or 2 adults + 2 children, manual or automatic available if specified when booking, includes substantial camper equipment and linens.
- Camper Iceland TYR A / THOR: Hilux/Isuzu D-Max/Nissan Navara or similar 4x4 truck camper categories. TYR A is automatic and more comfort-focused with toilet/warm water; THOR can be automatic or manual depending on assignment.
- GO Iceland lists a Toyota Hilux 4x4 Camper Automatic, but appears closely tied to the Rent.is/Flugvellir booking ecosystem. Compare terms carefully against booking directly with Rent.is.

Tradeoffs:
- Larger/taller vehicle, potentially more affected by wind.
- Manual versions require comfort with manual driving on gravel, hills, and rough roads.
- Camper body can be less pleasant on very rough tracks than a simpler SUV setup.

### Toyota Land Cruiser camper

Best fit when driving capability and compactness matter more than indoor camper space.

Known option:
- Go Campers Go 4x4 Cruiser Camper: automatic, 4WD, 2023-2026 Toyota Land Cruiser, sleeps 2, night heater, cooling box, gas stove/electric cooler/cooking gear, F-road allowed.

Tradeoffs:
- Usually only sleeps 2.
- Less standing/sitting/cooking space than a Hilux camper box.
- More SUV-like and easier to drive on rougher roads.

## Decision Factors

- Confirm the vehicle is explicitly permitted on F-roads, and ask for any excluded roads in writing.
- Confirm whether the rental permits specific roads of interest such as F35, F208, F225, F26, F210, F249, and roads 35/550/570.
- Assume many F-roads may not open until late June or early July. The June 13-27 timing means early-trip highland access may be limited.
- Treat river crossings as high-risk. Water damage is commonly excluded even from premium insurance packages.
- Get the strongest reasonable insurance package: CDW/SCDW, gravel protection, sand/ash protection, tire/windscreen coverage if available, roadside assistance, and extra driver if needed.
- Read exclusions carefully: wind-damaged doors, undercarriage, tires, water/river damage, towing/recovery, off-road driving, closed roads, and unauthorized F-roads are commonly excluded.
- For June camper life, prioritize heater, blackout curtains, bedding quality, indoor cooking, fridge/cooler, water tank, auxiliary battery, USB/12V/230V, campsite power cable, and real storage.
- Choose automatic if the price gap is acceptable unless all likely drivers are very comfortable driving manual on gravel, hills, and in wind.
- Check pickup/drop-off logistics at Keflavik, flight-time fit, after-hours pickup, shuttle details, deposit/hold, age requirements, license requirements, and whether an International Driving Permit is needed.
